_The BRC Analytics team_

We spent last week at the [ASTMH 2026 Annual Meeting](https://www.astmh.org/) in
National Harbor, Maryland (November 18–22), where the BRC Analytics and Galaxy
teams presented a symposium on archive-scale pathogen genomics — from curated
NCBI genome packages and TACC compute to agentic analysis, verifiable AI, and
the next phase of BRC Analytics.

Several audience members asked us to make the slides available, so here they
are — five decks, hosted right here on the site, exactly as presented.

## The decks

Each deck opens in any browser:

- **[BRC-Analytics — AI + Galaxy + Infrastructure](/learn/astmh-2026/astmh-intro-slides.html)**
  — _Anton Nekrutenko, Pennsylvania State University._ Symposium introduction:
  from curated NCBI genome packages and TACC compute to agentic biological
  discovery and integrated portals.
- **[Your Pathogen Question. A Reproducible Analysis.](/learn/astmh-2026/astmh-malaria-slides.html)**
  — _Ananias A. Escalante, Temple University._ Malaria as a case study for BRC
  Analytics — from a well-posed question to a five-taxon variant-calling run.
- **[New Technologies in BRC: Querying Entire SRA, Agentic Analysis, Revolutionary New Tooling](/learn/astmh-2026/astmh-tools-brc3-slides.html)**
  — _Anton Nekrutenko, Pennsylvania State University._ Archive-scale search
  with Logan, kmindex, LexicMap, disassembler, the Aeon evolutionary suite, and
  new precomputed data products.
- **[Verifiable AI for Pathogen Genomics](/learn/astmh-2026/astmh-ai-tools-slides.html)**
  — _Danielle Callan, Temple University._ AI you can check: agentic analysis on
  BRC Analytics.
- **[Human-Written, Agent-Readable](/learn/astmh-2026/astmh-gtn-ai-slides.html)**
  — _Scott Cain, Galaxy Project._ Grounding AI agents in the Galaxy Training
  Network — and running them on usegalaxy.org.
